# Amazon: All Marketplace Offers in One Table

A Tampermonkey userscript that collects all available offers for an Amazon product across supported Amazon marketplaces, including new and used items from all sellers, into one sortable table.

### Features
- View all available offers in one table
- Compare prices, shipping costs, and total costs
- View seller details, ratings, item conditions, and delivery information
- Sort and filter offers
- Open product and seller links directly
- Add offers to your cart
- Copy or export offers to CSV

Supports multiple Amazon marketplaces, including the Netherlands (NL), United States (US), Germany (DE), United Kingdom (UK), France (FR), and more.
